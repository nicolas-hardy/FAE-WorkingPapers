About the series

FAE Working Paper Series

The FAE Working Paper Series disseminates ongoing research by faculty, researchers, and affiliated scholars of the Facultad de Administración y Economía, Universidad Diego Portales.

Working papers are preliminary research outputs circulated to encourage academic discussion and exchange. Views expressed in individual papers are those of their authors.
