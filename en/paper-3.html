FAE Working Paper No. 3

How substitutable are high-skilled workers? The case of the expansion of tertiary education in Chile

Roberto Cases · Centro de Estudios Públicos

Adolfo Fuentes · Universidad Diego Portales

October 6, 2026

Download full paper

Abstract

This paper explores the implications of the expansion of tertiary education in Chile from 2010 to 2019, focusing primarily on how large firms substitute workers with varying qualifications and experience. Despite a significant increase in the share of tertiary-educated people aged 25-59 years old to 45 percent, there is no substantial decline in the wage premium associated with college-educated workers. Regarding occupations, we found a notable mismatch between educational attainment and job requirements, with most workers with higher vocational education overqualified, potentially leading to displacement by their college-educated counterparts. Then, we propose a novel model estimated using administrative data and find close-to-perfect substitutability between workers with higher vocational and college education. Finally, we emphasize the need to differentiate programs across educational levels to achieve more effective integration into the labor market.

JEL Classification: E24, J20, J31.
