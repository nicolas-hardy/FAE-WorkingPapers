FAE Working Paper No. 1

Forecasting Oil Prices Across the Distribution: A Quantile VAR Approach

Hilde C. Bjørnland, Nicolás Hardy and Dimitris Korobilis

We develop a Quantile Bayesian Vector Autoregression (QBVAR) to forecast real oil prices across different quantiles of the conditional distribution.

Read the paper
FAE Working Paper No. 0

Generalized Bayesian Composite Quantile Regression with an Application to Equity Premium Forecasting

Nicolás Hardy and Dimitris Korobilis

We develop generalized Bayesian composite quantile regression (CQR), which exponentiates the composite quantile loss directly and targets the same objective as frequentist CQR.
