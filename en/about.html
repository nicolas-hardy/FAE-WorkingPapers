FAE Working Paper Series

The FAE Working Paper Series disseminates ongoing research by faculty, researchers, and collaborators of the Faculty of Business and Economics at Diego Portales University.

Working papers present preliminary research results circulated to encourage academic discussion and exchange. The views expressed in each paper are the responsibility of its authors.
