Applied Economics Letters · Published online: 13 Aug 2026

Does external vulnerability predict sovereign rating-at-risk?

Mauricio G. Villena and María J. Quinteros

Abstract. Sovereign ratings are persistent, so rating-level regressions may miss changes in downside credit risk. We propose rating-at-risk: the probability and magnitude of future rating deterioration conditional on the inherited rating. In a panel of 68 emerging and developing economies, external vulnerability predicts 1- and 2-year downgrade risk and multi-year notch losses, with the signal concentrated in the upper tail of the vulnerability distribution. Macroeconomic controls attenuate pooled binary estimates but leave the within-country and cumulative notch-loss evidence intact. External vulnerability improves country-blocked validation modestly but not temporal validation over 2018–2023, so we interpret rating-at-risk as a transition-risk summary rather than a forecasting rule.

DOI: 10.1080/13504851.2026.2717404
