Forecasting Oil Prices Across the Distribution: A Quantile VAR Approach

Hilde C. Bjørnland, Nicolás Hardy and Dimitris Korobilis

25 de septiembre de 2026

We develop a Quantile Bayesian Vector Autoregression (QBVAR) to forecast real oil prices across different quantiles of the conditional distribution. The model allows predictor effects to vary across quantiles, capturing asymmetries that standard mean-focused approaches miss.

Documento de Trabajo FAE No. 0

Generalized Bayesian Composite Quantile Regression with an Application to Equity Premium Forecasting

Nicolás Hardy and Dimitris Korobilis

25 de septiembre de 2026

We develop generalized Bayesian composite quantile regression (CQR), which exponentiates the composite quantile loss directly and targets the same objective as frequentist CQR. The framework calibrates posterior uncertainty, accommodates shrinkage and variable selection, and avoids latent-variable augmentation.
