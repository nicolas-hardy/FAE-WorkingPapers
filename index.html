Generalized Bayesian Composite Quantile Regression with an Application to Equity Premium Forecasting

Nicolás Hardy and Dimitris Korobilis

25 September 2026

We develop generalized Bayesian composite quantile regression by exponentiating the composite quantile loss directly. The approach delivers a Gibbs posterior with calibrated uncertainty, supports shrinkage and variable selection, and avoids latent-variable augmentation.
