FAE Working Paper No. 0

Generalized Bayesian Composite Quantile Regression with an Application to Equity Premium Forecasting

Nicolás Hardy · Universidad Diego Portales

Dimitris Korobilis · University of Glasgow

25 September 2026

Download sample PDF coverDownload editable coverView on SSRNAll working papers

Abstract

Composite quantile regression is a robust and efficient estimator under heavy-tailed and contaminated errors. This paper develops a generalized Bayesian composite quantile regression framework that exponentiates the composite quantile loss directly, targeting the same objective as frequentist CQR. Posterior dispersion is governed by a learning rate calibrated to frequentist sandwich uncertainty. The framework accommodates normal and spike-and-slab LASSO priors, avoids latent-variable augmentation, and yields substantial computational gains. An application to equity premium forecasting illustrates the economic value of the resulting robust predictive estimates.

Keywords: Composite quantile regression; Gibbs posterior; Generalized Bayes; Learning rate calibration; Equity premium forecasting; Spike-and-slab priors.
